TermiNote - portable version
============================

This zip is the portable version of TermiNote for Windows 11 x64.

TO USE IT
1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like (a USB stick is fine).
2. Run TermiNote.exe.

PORTABLE MODE
The file "portable.txt" in this folder tells TermiNote to keep its data
(Markdown notes, settings.json, and recovery drafts) in this folder
instead of in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TermiNote. Do not delete portable.txt unless
you want TermiNote to go back to the normal data folder.

YOUR NOTES
Your notes live as plain Markdown files in the notes folder. You can
read, back up, or copy them to another computer. Notes stay on your
computer. Automatic update checks can be disabled in Settings.
